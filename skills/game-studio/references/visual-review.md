# Art and animation review

Use the approved character and environment references, rejected directions,
audience and requested actions as the acceptance criteria. Inspect the reference
before proposing a fix. Technical rendering success does not establish fidelity.

Compare a neutral full-body render and facial close-up with the approved identity:
silhouette, head/body proportions, crest, expression, eye placement, beak shape,
palette, contour weight and shading. Check assembled parts at animation extremes
for gaps, double outlines, detached pivots, clipping and import rescaling. For
sprite atlases, verify runtime texture dimensions match the authored crop grid.

Inspect consecutive frames on both sides of every animation-state and sprite or
pose substitution boundary, including takeoff, landing, turns and blink drawings.
An isolated cycle and its extreme poses can pass while a one-frame handoff still
snaps. Check silhouette, pivot position and attached parts at the exact switch;
accept the correction only after the rendered handoff is continuous.

Review staged depth through relative camera movement, overlap, contact shadows,
perspective and actual occlusion. A character entering a door should pass behind
its edge progressively; a scene fade alone does not demonstrate entry. Preserve
travel direction and velocity across cuts. Keep foreground framing out of the
important acting area. Inspect resolution and silhouette clarity at final size.

For flight, inspect anticipation, downstroke/recovery, wing attachment, secondary
crest/tail motion, foot tuck, landing contact and settle. For walking, check stance
foot slip. For speech, inspect connected upper/lower beak motion, readable shape
changes, silent closures, gaze and blink transitions. Do not infer lip sync from
a waveform merely existing: compare rendered facial states with timing cues.

Retain a full-sequence storyboard and dense short contact sheets around flight,
landing, doorway crossings and dialogue. Inspect actual playback when a tool
supports it. Report which evidence was viewed and whether audio was actually
auditioned. Assign concrete defects with frame/time, expected behavior, owner and
the next verification needed; accept only after the corrected render is checked.
